## Business Workflow
This project focuses on generating pitch emails for indie games.

## User
A game developer or studio founder who wants to present their game idea to potential investors or publishers.

## Input
A short game concept, including genre, setting, and key features.

## Output
A professional pitch email that introduces the game and invites further discussion.

## Why this task is Valuable
Game developers often need to communicate their ideas clearly and persuasively to investors or publishers. Writing effective pitch emails can be time-consuming and requires both creativity and business communication skills. A GenAI workflow can help generate a strong first draft quickly, while still allowing human refinement.
