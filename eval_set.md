# Evaluation Set

## Case 1: Normal case
**Input:**  
A 2D pixel-art platformer set in a post-apocalyptic city. The levels are procedurally generated, and the game supports co-op multiplayer with a retro-style soundtrack throughout.
**Good output should:**  
It should highlight what makes the experience distinct, including the visual style, the replayability from procedural generation, and the appeal of co-op, without just listing those things mechanically. 

## Case 2: Normal case
**Input:**  
A cozy farming simulation set in a magical forest. Players tend crops, befriend animal companions, and craft items that change with the seasons. The game is designed to feel slow, warm, and rewarding.
**Good output should:**  
Match the tone of the game, calm and inviting rather than aggressive or overly sales-driven. It should speak to why the cozy genre has a strong and loyal audience, and frame the seasonal and crafting systems as reasons players will keep coming back.

## Case 3: Normal case
**Input:**  
A narrative mystery game set in 1920s Shanghai. The story branches based on player choices, the dialogue is fully voiced, and the art is drawn from historical references of the era.
**Good output should:**  
Lead with the setting and atmosphere, since that is the most distinctive element. It should explain why the historical backdrop and branching narrative are meaningful rather than just visually interesting, and position the game within the current appetite for story-driven experiences. 

## Case 4: Edge case
**Input:**  
A dark narrative game about memory, identity, and truth.
**Good output should:**  
Organize abstract ideas into a clear pitch and avoid being too vague. This is a harder input because there is no concrete genre, mechanic, or setting to anchor the email. A good output finds a way to make the themes feel specific and interesting rather than generic. A weak output will produce something that could describe almost any indie game.

## Case 5: Likely failure / hallucination
**Input:**  
A game inspired by Persona and Danganronpa with time loops and multiple perspectives.
**Good output should:**  
Reference the inspirations accurately and use them to signal audience and tone, without misrepresenting what those games are or inventing features that were not mentioned. This case is likely to produce hallucinations because the model may add gameplay mechanics, story details, or market claims that were never in the input. Any output here should be reviewed carefully before use.
